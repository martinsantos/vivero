The Echeveria Elegans, also known as the Mexican Snowball, is a popular succulent prized for its symmetrical rosette shape and soft, pale green leaves. It's a relatively small succulent, typically growing up to 4 inches in diameter, making it ideal for small spaces or as part of a larger succulent arrangement.

This plant is easy to care for, requiring minimal watering and thriving in bright, indirect light. Its drought-tolerant nature makes it a great choice for beginners or those who prefer low-maintenance plants.
